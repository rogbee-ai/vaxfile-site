Moving from the UK to Australia: your child's vaccinations

The UK and Australian schedules cover mostly the same diseases, but Australia gives some vaccines at different ages, includes a few the UK doesn't, and links vaccination to childcare and family payments. The most important step: get your child's UK record onto the Australian Immunisation Register (AIR).

Childcare and family payments

  • No Jab No Play. Victoria, New South Wales, Queensland, South Australia and Western Australia require children to be up to date (or on an approved catch-up schedule) to enrol in childcare or kindergarten. The ACT, Tasmania and the Northern Territory have no enrolment rule, but unvaccinated children can be kept home during an outbreak.
  • No Jab No Pay. To get Family Tax Benefit Part A supplements and Child Care Subsidy, your child must meet the immunisation requirements.
  • In both cases, the AIR is what counts.

Getting UK doses onto the AIR

  • A GP or other vaccination provider adds overseas vaccinations to your child's AIR record.
  • Bring the red book (Personal Child Health Record) or a GP printout showing vaccine names and dates.
  • Catch-up vaccines on the National Immunisation Program are free for everyone under 20.

Key differences at a glance

Which UK schedule your child had

  • Born before September 2022: MMR at 1 year and 3 years 4 months. No chickenpox vaccine.
  • Born September 2022 – June 2024: MMR at 1 year, MMRV at 3 years 4 months.
  • Born July – December 2024: MMR at 1 year, MMRV at 18 months and 3 years 4 months, and a 4th 6-in-1 at 18 months.
  • Born from January 2025: MMRV at 1 year and 18 months, 4th 6-in-1 at 18 months.

What your child may need after the move

  • MenACWY. UK toddlers don't get it, so expect a catch-up dose. It's free.
  • Chickenpox if your child was born before September 2022. Free catch-up up to age 20.
  • 18-month boosters (DTaP, Hib) if your child was born before July 2024. Hib catch-up stops at age 5.
  • Flu every year for under-5s, free.
  • MenB: your child likely had it in the UK. Nothing extra needed.
