Moving between countries

Schedules differ more than you might expect. Some countries vaccinate against chickenpox, MenB or flu and others don't, and the same vaccine can be due at different ages.

Moving from the US to the UK? See what carries over and what your child may be offered.

When you move, take your child's vaccination records to your new doctor or health centre. They compare them with the local schedule and offer any missing doses. Each country page explains how this works there.
