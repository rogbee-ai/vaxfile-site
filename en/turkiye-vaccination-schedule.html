If your child was born earlier

6-in-1 vaccine (since April 2025)

Since April 2025, the 6-in-1 vaccine (DaBT-İPA-Hib-HepB) has replaced the 5-in-1 vaccine (DaBT-İPA-Hib) in the calendar, combining it with the hepatitis B vaccine. Older vaccination cards usually show the 5-in-1 vaccine and separate hepatitis B doses.

Second chickenpox dose (since September 2026)

The second chickenpox dose at 48 months was added on 1 September 2026. Children aged 49 to 72 months who haven't had it are offered a catch-up dose.

Moving away from Türkiye with children

  • Take the vaccination card (aşı kartı) with you. Your doctor in the new country checks it against the local schedule and offers any missing doses.
  • Your new country may schedule vaccines differently. For example, Germany and the Netherlands don't routinely give BCG, oral polio or hepatitis A to children, and Germany requires proof of measles vaccination for Kita and school.

Questions

What is the extra MMR dose at 9 months?

The 2026 calendar includes an extra early MMR dose (KKK ek doz) at 9 months. The two routine MMR doses follow at 12 months and at 4 years (48 months).

Is there a second chickenpox dose?

Yes. Since 1 September 2026, a second chickenpox dose is given at 48 months. Children aged 49 to 72 months who haven't had it are offered a catch-up dose.

Is VaxFile available in Türkiye?

No. VaxFile isn't offered in the App Store or Google Play in Türkiye. It includes the Turkish schedule for families who have moved from Türkiye and want to keep their children's records together.

What if my child had vaccines in Türkiye and we moved?

Take the vaccination card (aşı kartı) to your doctor in the new country. They check it against the local schedule and offer any missing doses.
